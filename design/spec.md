# Violet Glass design specification

The two supplied reference images define the skin. DM Sans, white icons, translucent violet glass and magenta rim lighting apply throughout. The showcase is a minimal #070416 cinema stage.

Desktop requires a player wider than 900px and a viewport wider than 1024px. A floating 90px dock contains time/seek, 52/70/52px transport orbs, volume and utilities, in source order. Tablet/mobile uses centered 76/114/76px orbs above a 78px pill dock. Compact phones scale the orbs and dock to fit inside the 16:9 canvas.

The timeline is a straight 8px track, buffered fill, neon gradient played fill and 20px white glowing thumb. Settings has speed and quality submenus, a synchronized captions toggle, close action and downward caret. Options reflect actual media availability.

Controls retain keyboard operation, visible focus, announcements and gestures. Motion respects reduced motion; glass has contrast and transparency preference fallbacks.
