Darkroom

A player developed under safelight. The controls ride on a strip of 35 mm film, and the film advances through the gate while the video plays.

01Design notes

Most custom players float a blurred glass panel over the picture. Darkroom grounds the controls on a solid strip of film base instead. Its two sprocket rails are real holes, so the picture shows through them, and while the video plays the rails roll forward like film through a projector gate. Pause, and the film stops.

Amber, the colour of a darkroom safelight, marks everything you can act on. The timeline is cut into frames, and the part you've watched glows from safelight red into amber, as if it had been exposed. Hover the timeline and the preview frame arrives in a paper slide mount with its timecode printed on the card. Tooltips are strips of masking tape.

Because the controls sit on a solid strip, they keep the same contrast over a white sky and a black night scene. Switch the footage above to see it.

02Icon set

Drawn for this skin on a 24 px grid with a 2 px stroke and square caps. Transport glyphs are solid, and active states fill in, so state never depends on colour alone.

    03Built for everyone

    • Readable over any frame. Text measures 16:1 and the amber accent 10:1 on the strip, and still 14:1 and 9:1 with a white frame behind it.
    • Easy to tap. Every control is at least 44 px on touch screens.
    • Visible focus. One amber ring with a dark halo on buttons, slider thumbs and menu items.
    • Calm on request. With reduced motion on, the film stands still and menus cut.
    • Fits the player, not the page. Container queries rearrange the strip from 20 rem to full screen.
    • Any language. All labels come from Video.js i18n, and the layout mirrors for RTL.
